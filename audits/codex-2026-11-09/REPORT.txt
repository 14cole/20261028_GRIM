Overall disposition

The principal correctness and robustness findings are supported. C-1, C-2,
C-3, C-4, C-5, R-1, the controlled R-2 race, and R-3a now have independent
runtime evidence. Several performance opportunities also reproduced, but
their gains are workload-dependent. Do not treat the proposed fixes as
validated production changes or add their individual savings together.

Every one of the 54 atomic findings has a disposition in coverage.csv.
Grouping headings C-6, R-3 and M-3 are not counted as separate findings.
The results directory contains 127 standalone case records plus composite
correctness, robustness, profiling and microbenchmark results. Most standalone
records contain a co-polarized solve; this count is not a count of assertions.

Environment and measurement method

Linux, Python 3.12.14, NumPy 2.3.5, SciPy 1.17.0, gcc 14.2.0; four-CPU cgroup
quota and 16 GiB cgroup memory. Timed case processes were pinned to four allowed
CPUs. OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 and OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 were set before imports;
explicit auto profiles and BLAS comparisons may change these limits.
All shipped Linux native C sources were built with their supplied builders
and passed the builders' export/load checks. Windows DLLs were not executed.

Performance comparisons ran sequentially, in fresh processes. The principal
native/fallback, W-rule, coarse-near and BLAS comparisons have three repetitions.
Other timing measurements are explicitly single cases, not confidence intervals.
Profiled runs are separate from unprofiled timing comparisons. Inclusive stage
and cProfile sums overlap and must not be added as exclusive CPU costs.

run_case.py's cpu field is PARENT process CPU time. It excludes child-process
CPU. rss_peak is parent ru_maxrss. For compressed-worker memory comparisons,
use metadata.runtime_profile.sampled_peak_process_tree_rss_bytes, sampled at
250ms, which may miss brief peaks and double-count shared pages. Earlier chat
figures of 0.66/0.91 GB for 5120/6144 compressed were parent-only; corrected tree
peaks are 1.168/1.492 GB. GB and GiB are explicitly distinguished below.

Highest-priority findings for Claude

1. C-1: fix the TE open-TYPE 2 guard across mixed scenes.
   Independent certified runs gave a 3.0667 dB VV discrepancy between an open
   strip+dielectric and its thin-plate comparison. Both certified. The open
   result's published VV maximum dB error estimate was 0.007628 dB.
   Retain valid TYPE 2/3/4 junction behavior. Do not merely broaden a degree
   count without testing partial coatings, sheet mixtures and touching ends.

2. C-2: reject nonfinite solutions, but preserve a valid zero RHS.
   A zero 3x3 system returned three NaNs and zero backward/residual metrics.
   The proposed np.where expression in audit line 74 assigns infinity to the
   valid A=I,b=0 solution because ri=den=0. Handle this case explicitly.
   np.fmax ignores NaNs; it is not an invalid-result detector.

3. C-3: remove global-RNG dependence without a process-global reseeding race.
   Actual compressed 1200-unknown two-circle runs changed global RNG state;
   seeds 1..6 produced condition estimates 1503.679 or 1471.679 in this fixture.
   Dense runs were stable and preserved the RNG. Claude's 3.4x spread was not
   recreated, and estimator spread is not proof of underestimation relative
   to an exact condition number. No gate-threshold crossing was demonstrated.

4. C-4: fix modal extension convergence scaling.
   Radius 0.1 m cone-cylinder, 3 GHz, N=37 per primitive, mode_tol=1e-13: automatic
   selection failed at pair (0,3), order 192, relative change 0.000185. Explicit
   cap 31 succeeded with 20 modes. A neighboring N=36 case succeeded both ways.
   An initial smaller-radius fixture did not trigger extension; both attempts
   are retained so absence of failure is not mistaken for a refutation.

5. C-5: broaden the documented exposure and preserve wavelength safeguards.
   N=-1 bypasses the positive-N density floor with enforce_mesh_density=True;
   a 10x10 square at wavelength 1 retained panels with |k|L=2pi. The claim that
   enforcement always guarantees |k|L<=1.57 is false. Lossy meshing uses |n|,
   resolving the original report's uncertainty about Re(k) versus |k|.
   Touching S relative error at 150deg rose to 0.4066 at |k|L=20, versus ~1.8e-13
   at |k|L=3. Far-pair errors depend strongly on orientation; our particular far
   pair did not recreate Claude's worst-case far-column numbers.

6. R-1/R-2/R-3a: retain reproducible concurrency and recovery tests.
   Three concurrent direct solves reproduced zero-GiB reservation failures.
   Controlled overlap in the actual HierarchicalFactor._lu left a permanent
   warnings error filter. This is an instrumented race reproduction, not an
   uninstrumented large-BOR demonstration; Python warning-context semantics
   vary by version. An isolated frequency-worker self-SIGKILL propagated
   BrokenProcessPool through the real compute_parallel path.

7. R-3g: the mixed closed/open TM resonance concern reproduced.
   At ka 2.4048255577 the condition estimate reached 3.098e7. The only reported
   warning concerned dangling endpoints; the formulation was the SLP Robin
   path. The absence of a closed-obstacle combined formulation is confirmed.

Performance claims requiring qualification

- S-9: auto versus off RHS compression gave no clear total-time improvement
  in the independent 181-angle sphere fixture. Exact cone probe productivity
  counts require the original geometry/options.
- S-11: 600-element BOR took 21.83 s at 1 GB budget, 23.01 s at .25 GB and 23.24 s at .06 GB.
  Smaller budgets lowered parent peak memory 1.62 GB to 1.12-1.14 GB, but did not
  improve speed. The stored fields were identical. No universal .25 GB optimum.
- S-13: 260642 bistatic samples used 289 MB/.499 s in normal storage versus
  102 MB/.569 s compact, in fresh processes. Memory savings reproduced; the
  reported 4x runtime benefit did not. Timings are single-run observations.
- S-16: two frequency workers each receive a CPU share. A two-worker cap alone
  does not establish idle cores; it is not equivalent to two execution threads.
- N-1: no blanket 5-11x slowdown. Results depend on electrical size and backend.
  Automatic was slower on some small fixtures but faster at 5120/6144. Below
  8192 it can also save meaningful memory. Parent-only CPU cannot establish
  total CPU ratios once tile worker processes are involved.

Memory findings

Dense runtime estimate versus measured parent peak, in GiB:
5120:1.001 versus 1.320;6144:1.356 versus 1.852;8448:2.383 versus 3.389.
The original report's 1.28/1.32 ratios did not prove a 1.35 margin violation.
This independent 6144 result does: ratio 1.366. Runtime pair admission separately
adds the partner matrix, but the event named total_peak_gib logs the estimate
WITHOUT that partner. Correct both forecasting and misleading telemetry.

Compressed first forecasts versus sampled process-tree peak, in decimal GB:
5120:2.994 versus 1.168;6144:3.259 versus 1.492;8448:1.183 versus .946.
This supports over-reservation and the backend-threshold discontinuity. It
is not sufficient to replace conservative budgets by observed mean RSS.
Correct workers priced but never launched before reducing safety reserves.

The BOR planning baseline was about 2.10 GB in the independent serial 60-element
case, not an invariant 2.8 GB floor. Options and worker allocations matter.
An isolated 6000x6000 synthetic low-rank kernel factorization added 1.190 matrix
units for HODLR versus 1.023 for LU, supporting the omitted ordered-copy cost.
The material-specific 3.5/2.4 multipliers remain unverified exact measurements.

Physics and numerical baseline

At ka 3, PEC/IBC/dielectric/sheet/coated sphere errors at 60 elements were
0.00613/0.01002/0.00299/0.01037/0.00466 dB, respectively, with approximately
second-order convergence across 30/60/120 elements. References used separate
analytic Mie routines in the repository, not the BEM assembly. These are
independent formulations, but not an external independently authored library.
2D sheet reference coefficients were separately implemented from interface
matching in analyze.py.

2D 360-panel PEC complex errors across ka .001,1.841,2.405,3.832,5.52,10 were
3.6e-9 to 9.3e-6. At ka 3, normal-contrast materials converged consistently;
annulus and two-layer complex errors at 240 panels per interface were 6.33e-5
and 3.38e-5. Weak contrast epsilon 1.0001 reproduced 0.06454 dB at ka 5.
Reactive BOR Z=50-200j degraded from 0.00846 dB at ka .01 to 0.08325 dB at ka 1e-4;
Z=37 ohm did not show the same degradation. The exact .11 dB claim differs.

Native/fallback 2D matrix differences were 4.11e-16 and 8.01e-17 relative.
BOR near Green kernels versus explicitly integrated 3D Green function agreed
within 4.2e-15 at gaps from .1 to 1e-7; native/fallback differences stayed below
9e-16. Units, translations, rotations and co-/single-polarization agreement
were about 1e-15 to 8e-15; frequency ordering was exact in the tested case.
Checkpoint round-trip was exact. Six repeated dense solves plateaued in RSS,
with no accumulating Python threads, file descriptors or children.

Additional smoke coverage included P2/P3, mixed precision, BOR tables,
single-precision tables, compressed BOR, workers 1/2, 12000 2D panels and
600 BOR elements. BOR tables/compressed fields agreed with streaming at
~1.7e-15/~3.2e-15; single-precision tables differed by 3.8e-9 relative.
These are smoke tests, not exhaustive qualification of every option.

What remains unproved, rather than silently omitted

coverage.csv identifies every item and its evidence. The narrow-resonance
C-6a hypothesis was not reproduced on three epsilon 16 disk cases; they agreed
with enlarged internal-index caps within ~2e-9 dB. C-6c/d remain error-accounting
or acceptance-scale risks without a demonstrated wrong released solution.
The exact C-6h kernel-level reduction-order example was not reproduced;
our worker/budget field comparisons were identical. Exact S-3 sample counts,
S-6 quantization gains, S-7 symmetry gains, the specific S-9 cone probe counts,
and M-3c material multipliers lack matching original benchmark fixtures.
The source mechanisms were reviewed and are distinguished from measurements.

Windows execution, Windows commit-charge accounting, shipped Windows DLL
behavior, actual SLURM/HPC end-to-end deployment, exhaustive narrow-resonance
searches, all junction topologies and every spill/checkpoint failure path
remain outside executed coverage. Linux tests cannot establish those results.
The original audit's entire numerical grid was not recreated cell for cell.
This is a complete disposition of its 54 findings with substantial independent
reproduction, not a claim that every possible solver behavior is verified.
